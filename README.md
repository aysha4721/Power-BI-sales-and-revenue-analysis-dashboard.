# Power-BI-sales-and-revenue-analysis-dashboard.
Interactive Power BI sales dashboard analyzing revenue trends, performance, and top products.
Why I created this dashboard?
To let stakeholders have an idea about how the business is going. about what products  sell well and bring the most revenue, daily sales trends, monthly trends, methods the company procured goods all of which can be filtered through dates so you can have an idea bout the previous + present year.
